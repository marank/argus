# Getting started

From signing in and your first request to the return.

## 1. Sign in

Open Argus in your browser and sign in with your username and password. If sign-in through the company directory is set up, these are your Windows credentials. If you cannot sign in, contact IT.

After signing in you see your dashboard with your requests and devices.

## 2. Request something

1. Choose **New request** on the left.
2. Enter a **Purpose**, e.g. "Training".
3. Choose **Temporary** or **Permanent** and set the period.
4. **On to selection**, search for the items you want or choose them via the categories, and add them.
5. **Submit request**.

More under [Requesting](./request).

## 3. Track the status

Under **My requests** you can see at any time where your request stands. Argus notifies you by email when

- your department or IT has decided,
- the devices are ready for pickup, with time and place,
- a return is due.

## 4. Pick up

Once the devices are ready, **Pick up** appears under **My requests**. Depending on what was agreed:

- **At the pickup terminal:** Tap your name, check the devices, sign. See [Pickup and return](./pickup#at-the-pickup-terminal).
- **At IT** or **Delivery**: IT hands over the devices and records the hand-out.

## 5. Return

You bring temporary devices to IT by the return date. Argus reminds you beforehand. If you need the devices longer, talk to IT. See [Returning](./pickup#returning).

## If something does not work

Open the device under **My devices** and choose **Report a problem**. See [Reporting a problem](./report-issue).

## Personal settings

In the user menu at the top right you choose language and theme, set up notifications, change your password and set up two-factor authentication. See [Account and settings](./account).
