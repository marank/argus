# Account and settings

You find all personal settings in the user menu at the top right.

## Signing in

Sign in with your username and password. If sign-in through the company directory is set up, these are your Windows credentials.

A sign-in lasts for a certain time, usually one working day. After that you sign in again.

After too many wrong passwords, sign-in is locked for 15 minutes. Contact IT if it cannot wait.

## Password

Under **Sign-in & security**:

- **With the company directory:** You change your password there, not in Argus.
- **With your own Argus account:** **Change password**, enter your current password, then the new one twice. Other sessions, e.g. on a second computer, are signed out afterwards.

A new password has at least 8 characters, does not consist of digits only and is not a common password.

If it says there that IT sets your password, contact IT for a new password.

## Two-factor authentication

Under **Sign-in & security** you can set up a second sign-in step. When signing in, you then also enter a 6-digit code.

| Method | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Authenticator app** (recommended) | Scan the QR code with an app such as Microsoft Authenticator. The app shows the code. |
| **Code by email** | Argus sends a code to your email address at every sign-in. |

During setup Argus shows ten **Backup codes** once. Keep them safe: each code replaces the code from the app or email once, e.g. when you do not have your phone.

If you have lost both your phone and your backup codes, IT resets your two-factor authentication after verifying your identity.

## Notifications

Argus notifies you by email and via the bell at the top right, e.g. when your request has been confirmed, the devices are ready or a return is due.

Under **Notifications** you decide for each kind whether you get it by email, in the app or not at all. Anything IT has switched off entirely does not appear there.

IT maintains your email address. If it is wrong, contact IT.

## Language and theme

- **Language:** Deutsch or English. Emails also come in this language.
- **Theme:** System, Light, Solarized, Dark or Retro. **System** follows the setting of your device.

Both apply on every device you sign in on.
