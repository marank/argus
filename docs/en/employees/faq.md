# FAQ

### How long does it take until I get my devices?

That depends on whether an approval is needed and whether the devices are in stock. You can see the status at any time under **My requests**. As soon as the devices are ready, you get the time and place.

### Can I change a request afterwards?

No. Cancel it and request again; **Request again** takes over your details. Alternatively, message IT; they can adjust quantities.

### I missed the pickup.

The devices remain ready for you. Pick them up or talk to IT about a new appointment.

### Can someone else pick up my devices?

Yes. At the pickup terminal that person chooses **Someone else** and signs, see [Pickup and return](./pickup#someone-else-picks-up). The devices stay assigned to you.

### I need a device longer than planned.

Contact IT. Only IT can extend the loan period.

### Why can't I see how many devices are available?

Only IT can see the stock. Request what you need; IT will get in touch if something is not available.

### My device is broken.

**My devices** → open the device → **Report a problem**. See [Reporting a problem](./report-issue).

### I get too many emails.

In the user menu under **Notifications** you can switch off individual emails, see [Account and settings](./account#notifications). The notifications in the app stay on unless you switch them off as well.

### I lost my phone with the authenticator app.

Sign in with a backup code. If you have none, IT resets your two-factor authentication after verifying your identity.

### How do I change my password?

See [Account and settings](./account#password).

### My sign-in has suddenly expired.

A sign-in only lasts for a certain time, usually one working day. Simply sign in again.
