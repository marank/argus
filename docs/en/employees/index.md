## How a request works

1. **Request:** Under **New request** you choose what you need and submit the request.
2. **Approval:** If your department has to agree, an approver decides first.
3. **Preparation:** IT confirms, prepares the devices and tells you the time and place.
4. **Pickup:** You pick up the devices and sign for the handover.
5. **Return:** You bring temporary devices back by the return date. Argus reminds you beforehand.

You get a notification at every step.
