# Pickup and return

As soon as your devices are ready, you get a notification with time and place. Under **My requests** the request then appears under **To do** with **Pick up**.

Depending on what was agreed, you get the devices in one of three ways:

| Way | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **At the pickup terminal** | You check the devices on a touch PC and sign, on your own or together with IT. |
| **At IT** | IT hands over the devices and records the handover. |
| **Delivery** | IT brings the devices to your workplace. |

## At the pickup terminal

1. **Tap your name:** Search for your name or your request number and tap it.
2. **Check the devices:** Look at the devices. If something is not right, e.g. a scratch or missing accessories, tap **Add note** on the device. Continue with **Everything checked**.
3. **Who collects?** Tap **I am {your name}**.
4. **Sign:** Sign in the field and tap **Sign and finish**.

You then get a confirmation listing all devices and the return date.

::: info Good to know
- After 2 minutes without input the terminal goes back to the start. Anything you entered is lost.
- Sometimes IT has already checked the devices with you. The terminal then starts directly with step 3.
:::

## Someone else picks up

If a colleague can pick up the devices for you, they choose **Someone else** at the terminal, search for themselves by name or employee number and sign. The devices stay assigned to you.

## Missed the pickup

The devices remain ready for you. Pick them up or agree on a new appointment with IT.

## Returning

You bring temporary devices to IT by the return date. The date is shown under **My devices**.

- Argus reminds you by email a few days beforehand.
- Once the date has passed, the request appears under **To do** with **Bring back**.
- If you need the devices longer, talk to IT. Only IT can extend the loan period.

IT records the return. You then get a confirmation of what was taken back and what is still outstanding.

You keep permanently handed-out devices until IT asks for them back or replaces them.
