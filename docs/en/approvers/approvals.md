# Approving or rejecting

For some categories, e.g. expensive devices, the department has to approve a request before IT handles it. This page is for approvers.

Everything else about Argus, e.g. how to request something yourself, is in the [Argus for employees](../employees/) section.

## Who approves

IT records for every department who approves. If a department has no approvers of its own, the approvers of the parent department decide. While someone is away, the [delegate](./delegation) an approver has set up decides as well.

As an approver you see the **Approvals** menu entry in the self-service. The number next to it shows how many requests are waiting for you. You also get an **Approval needed** notification.

## Approving or rejecting

1. Open **Self-service → Approvals**. The oldest request is at the top.
2. Open the request and review it: who, what, what for and for which period.
3. Decide:
   - **Approve**, optionally with a comment. The request goes to IT.
   - **Decline** with a reason for the employee. The reason is required.

After your decision the request disappears from your list. One approver's decision is enough. The employee is told the outcome by notification.

For a repeated request, Argus shows which earlier request it follows and why that one was rejected.

## When no approval is needed

A request goes straight to IT if

- no line comes from a category that needs approval,
- the employee is exempt from approvals,
- IT entered the request on behalf of the employee, or
- neither the department nor a parent department has approvers.

If you as an approver request something yourself, your request counts as approved.

## Questions

### A decision was wrong.

A decision cannot be undone. If you approved by mistake, contact IT; they can reject the request. After a rejection by mistake, the employee requests again with **Request again**.

### I am away.

Set up a delegate for the period yourself, see [Delegation while away](./delegation).

### I don't get any notifications.

In the user menu under **Notifications**, check whether **Approval needed** is switched on. You can always see open approvals from the number next to **Approvals**.
