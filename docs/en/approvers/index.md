## Your role

Some categories, e.g. expensive devices, need the department's approval. Such requests come to you first:

1. An employee of your department requests something.
2. You get the **Approval needed** notification; the request appears under **Self-service → Approvals**.
3. You approve or reject. Approved requests go to IT, and the employee is told the outcome.
