# Delegation while away

If you are on holiday or ill, set up a delegate yourself. During the period you set, the delegate decides on your department's requests, just like you.

You find everything for this under **Self-service → Delegation**.

## The page at a glance

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Whether someone is currently standing in for you, who and until when, or which delegation is planned next. Below it, the departments you approve for. |
| **I approve for** | Only if you are standing in for someone yourself: for whom and in which period. |
| **My delegations** | Running and planned delegations with the status **Running** or **Planned**. |
| **Past delegations** | The most recent ended delegations, collapsed. |

## Setting up a delegate

1. Click **Delegate approvals**.
2. Search for the delegate (at least two letters of the name).
3. Choose **From** and **Until**. The quick choice sets the end to 1, 2 or 3 weeks; **Until removed** leaves it open.
4. **Delegate**.

You can set up several delegations, e.g. for different periods or two people at the same time.

## Changing the period

**Change period** adjusts a delegation, e.g. when your holiday lasts longer. Once the delegation has started, only the end can be changed.

## Back early

- **I'm back** in the status section ends all running delegations immediately.
- **End** ends a single running delegation. It stays visible under **Past delegations**.
- **Remove** deletes a planned delegation that has not started yet.

## What the delegate can do during the period

- They see all requests you decide on under **Self-service → Approvals**. At the top it says whom they are standing in for.
- They get the **Approval needed** notification for new requests.
- They approve or reject. The request shows their name as the person who decided.

You can still decide yourself during this time. One person's decision is enough.

::: info Good to know
- A delegation starts today at the earliest. Before the first and after the last day, the delegate sees nothing.
- The delegate's own requests do not automatically count as approved, not even during the period.
- A delegate cannot set up further delegations themselves.
- The delegate must have an active account. If it is deactivated, the delegation ends.
- Approvers and people currently standing in for someone see the **Delegation** menu entry.
:::
