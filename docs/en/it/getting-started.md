# Getting started

The path of a request, from arrival to return, and where to look in your daily work.

## Your workspace

- **Dashboard**: What needs doing: to confirm, to prepare, ready for handover, overdue. Every number leads to the matching list. Adjust it with **Customize dashboard**, see [Dashboard and calendar](./dashboard-calendar).
- **Bell** at the top right: notifications, e.g. new requests for your team.
- **Scanner**: A scan on any page opens the device. See [Scanning](./scanning).

## A request from start to finish

1. **Arrival**: The request appears under **IT operations → Requests** with the status **Submitted**. Your team receives a notification.
2. **Confirm**: Open the request, check availability, **Confirm**. If something is missing, reduce the quantity or order more.
3. **Allocate devices**: Scan devices or use **Allocate all automatically**. The devices are then reserved.
4. **Finish preparation**: Choose the handover type, date and pickup point. The employee is notified.
5. **Handover**: The employee signs at the pickup terminal, or you record the handover with **Hand out**.
6. **Return**: For temporary requests, you take the devices back with **Take back**, defective ones with the **Damaged** checkbox ticked.

The details are under [Handling requests](./requests).

## Shortcuts for everyday work

| Situation | How |
| --- | --- |
| Someone needs a device off the shelf right now | **Quick assignment** on the device page |
| Provide several specific devices for someone | **Direct hand-out** in the menu |
| The device is already with the employee but not recorded | **Mark as handed out**, record the devices later |
| Device defective, replacement needed | **Replace** on the device page; the device name moves along |
| Battery replaced | **Record service** on the device page |
| New devices delivered | **Goods receipt** on the order, scan the serial numbers |

## Adding new devices

- One at a time: **Devices → New device**
- Many of one model: **Scan in multiple**
- From a list: **Import**, see [Importing devices](./device-import)
- From an order: automatically at [goods receipt](./procurement#goods-receipt)

Argus assigns inventory numbers and device names automatically if you leave the fields empty.

## What you are allowed to see

Your [roles](./administration/roles) determine which areas you see and can change. If you are missing an area, contact an administrator.
