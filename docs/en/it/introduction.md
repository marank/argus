# What is Argus?

Argus is the IT department's IT asset management. It brings together the inventory, employees' requests, the preparation and hand-out of devices, and procurement.

## What Argus covers

| Area | What for |
| --- | --- |
| **Self-service** | Employees request devices, software and consumables, track progress and see their devices and licenses. |
| **Approvals** | Departments approve requests before IT handles them, if the category requires it. |
| **Requests** | IT confirms, plans the pickup, allocates devices, hands them out and takes them back. |
| **Pickup terminal** | Employees pick up prepared devices at a touch PC and sign for the handover. |
| **Devices** | Every device with inventory number, serial number, location, holder and a complete service log. |
| **Consumables** | Stock per storage location with minimum quantities and movements. |
| **Licenses** | Software licenses with seats and term, assigned to employees or devices. |
| **Problem reports** | Employees report defects, damage and losses; IT handles them. |
| **Procurement** | Missing devices and material turn into orders; the goods receipt creates devices and allocates them right away. |
| **Master data** | Manufacturers, suppliers, categories, models, locations, departments, employees, roles and number sequences. |

You can switch off individual modules in the [settings](./administration/settings). Switched-off areas disappear from the interface.

Which areas someone sees depends on their [roles](./administration/roles). An area without permission is hidden.

## Using Argus

- **Scanning:** In the IT views, a barcode scanner works everywhere. A scanned inventory number, serial number, IMEI or EAN opens the matching device or model directly. See [Scanning](./scanning).
- **Live updates:** Lists, dashboards and the counters in the menu update themselves when someone else changes something. While a dialog is open, the page waits.
- **Language and theme:** In the user menu you choose German or English and a theme (System, Light, Dark and others).
