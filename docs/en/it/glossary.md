# Terms

The most important terms in Argus, grouped by topic.

## Requests

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Request** | A person's need for devices, software or consumables. A request can be temporary (a loan) or permanent. Employees submit it in self-service; IT can also create it on behalf of others. |
| **Line** | One line of a request, e.g. "1 × headset, any model" or "2 × toner cartridge". |
| **Approval** | The sign-off by the person's own department. Only needed for categories that require approval. |
| **Confirmation** | IT's acceptance of the request. Only after that does IT plan the pickup and the devices. |
| **Allocation** | A specific device that IT earmarks for a line. The device is then **Reserved**. |
| **Handover (hand-out)** | Handing the devices out to the person. It is recorded, optionally with a signature at the pickup terminal. |
| **Direct hand-out** | IT prepares specific devices from stock for a person, without a request and without approval. |
| **Custom request** | A line that is not in the catalog and is described in free text. IT assigns a model to it. |
| **Return** | IT taking back devices that were handed out. |

## Inventory

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Device** | An individually recorded piece of hardware with an inventory number, e.g. a laptop, smartphone, headset or MDE. |
| **Inventory number** | The permanent identifier of a device. It stays with the device for its whole life. |
| **Device name** | The name of a device at the place where it is used, e.g. the hostname of a PC or the name of an MDE in the MDM. The name belongs to that place: when a device is replaced, the replacement takes over its name. |
| **Model** | A manufacturer's device type, e.g. "Jabra Evolve2 65". Every device belongs to a model, every model to a category. |
| **Category** | A group of models, e.g. "Headsets" or "Notebooks". Categories determine whether devices are managed individually, as a quantity or as a license, and whether approval is required. |
| **Consumables** | Material tracked by quantity per storage location, e.g. ink cartridges or cables. |
| **License** | A software license with a number of seats and a term. It is assigned to employees or devices. |
| **Service log** | The life history of a device: hand-outs, returns, repairs, maintenance, inspections and notes. |
| **Problem report** | A reported problem with a device, e.g. a defect, damage or a loss. |

## Procurement

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Demand** | What is missing to fulfill open requests or to keep consumables at their minimum stock. |
| **Purchase order** | An order placed with a supplier. On goods receipt, it turns into devices or stock. |

## General

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Number sequence** | A pattern for consecutive numbers. Argus uses it to assign inventory numbers, device names, request numbers and order numbers. See [Number sequences](./administration/master-data#number-sequences). |
| **Team** | A group of IT staff to whom requests and problem reports are assigned automatically, e.g. by category. |

## People and accounts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Employees** | Everyone who uses Argus in self-service: requesting, viewing their own devices, reporting problems. |
| **IT staff** | Users with roles that give access to areas of IT operations. |
| **Administrators** | Users with full rights, including settings and role management. |
| **Approvers** | Employees who approve the requests of their department. |
| **Delegate** | Someone who decides on behalf of an approver for a period of time. Approvers enter their delegate themselves. |
| **Pickup terminal account** | An account for a shared touch PC where employees pick up prepared devices and sign for them. |
